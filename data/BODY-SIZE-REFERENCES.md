# S–XXL body-size references

Checked 20 September 2026 against official Nike size guides. These numerical ranges are external body-circumference references, not approved Vanta Noir measurements. They supplement, and never overwrite, merchant-entered garment charts or the existing four sample references.

Tops/outerwear use chest or bust plus waist. Bottoms use waist and hips. Matching sets show all three; both pieces require sample confirmation. Dresses and bodysuits use bust/waist/hip references. Underwear has its own range. Accessories must not receive apparel chest charts.

Data preserves the source inch ranges; the interface converts to centimetres using exactly 2.54 cm/in and rounds for display. It does not infer sleeve length, garment length, inseam, stretch, ease or missing XXL sampling specifications. Different cuts do not justify inventing body-size ranges.
