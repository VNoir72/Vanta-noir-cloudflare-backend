# Vanta Noir returns and exchanges — proposed policy

Draft for owner approval. Not published to customers. Proposed business commitments: 14 days to request a change-of-mind return, 7 days to send it after instructions, and refunds initiated within 7 business days of receipt and inspection. Confirm that fulfilment can meet these commitments before publishing. This policy is written for the catalogue (streetwear, matching sets, activewear, outerwear and accessories), not an assumption about the owner's personal wardrobe.

## Customer-facing draft

We want your Vanta Noir pieces to feel right. If the fit or style is not right for you, contact **vantanoir.xyz@gmail.com within 14 calendar days of delivery** to request a return or exchange. Include your order number, the item and your preferred resolution. We will send return instructions and the correct return address. Please send the item within 7 calendar days of receiving those instructions. Delays caused by us will not count against your return window.

### Condition of returned pieces

For a change-of-mind or size return, clothing and accessories must be unused, unwashed, unaltered and in their original condition, with attached tags and any included accessories. Trying clothing on indoors to check the fit is fine; wearing it outside or for a workout is not. Please avoid makeup, perfume, deodorant marks and other damage. Use the original packaging if available, or secure protective packaging.

Matching sets and multipacks sold as one item should be returned complete for a change-of-mind return. If only one component is faulty, contact us so we can arrange an appropriate remedy without unnecessarily requiring unaffected pieces to be returned.

### Underwear and hygiene-sensitive items

Change-of-mind returns of boxer briefs, underwear or other items sold with a hygiene seal are accepted only while that seal is intact and the item is unworn. Do not remove a hygiene seal until you intend to keep the item. We will identify these conditions on the product page before purchase. These conditions do not remove your rights where an item is faulty, incorrect or not as described.

Personalised items made to your instructions are excluded from our voluntary change-of-mind policy where this is disclosed before purchase. Faulty or incorrectly personalised items remain eligible for an appropriate remedy. Sale items otherwise follow the same returns policy.

### Wrong, damaged or faulty items

Please contact us as soon as you notice a problem, with your order number and a description. Photos can help us resolve it faster where practical, but are not a condition of your legal rights. We will arrange an appropriate repair, replacement or refund in accordance with applicable law and cover reasonable return costs for confirmed faults, incorrect items or items not as described. The 14-day change-of-mind window and original-condition requirements do not restrict these rights.

### Exchanges and return delivery

Exchanges depend on availability. If your requested size or colour is unavailable, we will offer an eligible refund rather than require store credit. For change-of-mind returns, you pay return delivery; for size exchanges, you also pay the new delivery charge, which we will confirm before you agree. Original delivery charges are not refunded for a voluntary change-of-mind return unless required by law. We do not charge a restocking fee.

Follow the return instructions, pack the item securely and keep proof of posting. If we arrange collection, we will explain the collection process. Please do not send items to an address on a parcel without confirming the return address with us.

### Refunds

After receiving and inspecting the return, we will confirm the outcome and initiate an approved refund within **7 business days**, using the original payment method where possible. If that method is unavailable, we will agree a suitable alternative with you. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to credit the funds. We will explain any rejected return and how to contact us about the decision.

### Cancellations and preorders

Contact us promptly to cancel an order before dispatch. A standard order cancelled before dispatch receives a full refund. After dispatch, the applicable return process applies. Preorder timing will be shown before purchase. If we cannot meet the stated dispatch window, we will contact you with the revised timing and offer cancellation and a refund if you do not wish to wait. Any special terms for personalised work must be disclosed before ordering and remain subject to applicable law.

### Your rights

This policy is in addition to your rights under applicable Nigerian consumer law. It does not exclude or limit rights concerning faulty goods, misleading descriptions or other rights that cannot legally be waived.

## Publication checklist

- Owner approves the 14-day / 7-day / 7-business-day commitments and delivery-cost allocation.
- Verify support inbox monitoring, return address, refund access and exchange handling.
- Only describe hygiene seals on products that actually use them; disclose exclusions before checkout.
- Publish consistently in Store Settings → Returns policy, Shipping & Returns and relevant product disclosures, replacing prior policy text consistently once approved.
- Nigerian legal review is recommended before adoption; this draft is not a guarantee against claims.
